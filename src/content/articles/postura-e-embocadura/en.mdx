Before worrying about speed or the number of notes, it is important to develop a comfortable posture and a consistent embouchure.

## Hold the instrument without tension

The ocarina should remain stable in your hands, but without the need to squeeze it.

The goal is to allow your fingers to move freely.

## The embouchure

The position of the lips directly influences the quality of the sound.

Seek a natural position and avoid excessive tension in the mouth.

# Getting to Know the Ocarina Notes

Before studying scales and exercises, it is important to become familiar with the notes that can be produced by the ocarina.

In this article, we will look at the relationship between musical notes and the fingerings of the instrument.

## The Range of the Instrument

A 12-hole ocarina has a range that allows you to play a variety of melodies.

The arrangement of the notes depends on the model and tuning of the instrument.

## A First Contact

The goal at this stage is not to memorize the entire range.

Start by recognizing the most commonly used notes and associate each one with its fingering.

After learning the basic notes, we can begin working with scales.
